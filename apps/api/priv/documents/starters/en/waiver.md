# Participation and assumption of risk

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer check it before you use it.**

I declare that:

- I take part in the box's classes and activities of my own free will.
- I know that exercise, especially with weights and at high intensity, carries a risk of injury, and I accept that risk as far as the law allows.
- I follow the coaches' instructions, use the equipment properly and stop exercising if I feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.
- I tell the coach before class about any injury, condition or change in my health that affects exercise.
- I have handed in, or will hand in, a doctor's health certificate as the law and the box require.

This declaration does not limit the box's liability for intent or gross negligence, nor my rights as a consumer.

I sign electronically, having read the text.
