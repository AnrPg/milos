# How we process your personal data

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer check it before you use it.**

## Controller

The box is the controller (name, address and contact details as shown on its profile). Palee processes the data on the box's behalf, as its processor.

## What data and why

- Account and contact details: for your subscription, bookings and updates (performance of a contract).
- Payments and receipts: for the box's tax obligations (legal obligation).
- Health certificate: only its dates and file, to establish that you may exercise (legal obligation, GDPR art. 9(2)). Only staff with the matching permission see it.
- Document signatures: the text you signed, the time, the IP address and the device, as proof of acceptance.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you train with the box and afterwards for as long as the law requires (e.g. tax records, proof of accepted terms). The health certificate is deleted once it is no longer needed.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and may complain to the data protection authority.
