# Terms of membership and house rules

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer check it before you use it.**

## Subscription and bookings

- A subscription package is valid for the period and the visits it states.
- Bookings and cancellations are made in the app, within the limits the box sets.
- A cancellation after the late-cancellation limit counts as a visit.

## Payments

- A subscription is paid before it starts, unless agreed otherwise.
- A purchase at a distance carries the 14-day right of withdrawal, as described on the purchase page.

## Space and safety

- We respect the coaches, the other members and the equipment, and put equipment back.
- The box may refuse anyone who puts themselves or others at risk.

## Changes

The box announces changes to these terms. Material changes ask for a new acceptance.
