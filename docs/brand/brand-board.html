thumoa
The spirit that moves you. · Η ψυχή που σε κινεί.

From the ancient Greek thumos (θυμός), the spirited part of the soul: drive, heart, the courage to come back tomorrow. Thumoa is the club where people who love to move find their people.

01 · The mark

The dancer: a "u", an "o", a person, a heart

One dot and one asymmetric stroke. Read it as the u and o of the name, as a person moving with one arm raised higher, or as hands cupping a heart. The raised arm is the thumos: the part of you that lifts first.

The marker test: a child should be able to draw it with one marker in under five seconds. No gradient, no outline, no inner detail. The last tile is literally drawn "by marker".

02 · Wordmark & rules

A dot above the "u", echoing the head

thumoa
thumoa
  • Always lowercase. The name is a friend, not an institution.
  • One colour per lockup. The dot may take Pomegranate when the letters are Ink.
  • The raised arm always points up and left of the head, so the figure lifts, never falls.
  • The wordmark should become a custom-drawn logotype on the Commissioner skeleton. This board uses the font as a stand-in.

A · Pomegranate & Chalk (recommended)

Warm, human, Mediterranean. Red reads as heart and spirit rather than danger thanks to the warm chalk ground. Owns a colour no fitness peer owns.

B · Ember (today's app)

Continuity with the current Milos tokens. Risk: sits next to Strava's orange, the most famous colour in the category.

C · Breath-led

Rides the 2026 "Transformative Teal" forecast. Calm and wellbeing-first, but less spirited. Better for a yoga brand than for thumos.

04 · Typography

Commissioner: a Greek-designed variable face with a "flair" axis

Aa Αα Ωω

By Greek type designer Kostas Bartsokas. Full Greek and Latin, so the brand speaks both languages in one family. The flair axis flares the stroke endings for headlines and campaigns, and drops to zero for calm interface text. One family, two moods: thumos and care.

07 · Motion

The head bounces once, like a breath before the lift

  • Signature motion: the dot lifts and lands. Used for loading, check-in success and kudos.
  • Celebration: the dot turns Sun for one beat on a PR, and is otherwise never coloured differently.
  • Respect reduced motion: the dot simply appears.
08 · Why this direction

Grounded in where brands are heading

Wordmarks and custom type carry identity

Icons are becoming secondary, and brands invest in custom type. Hence the dotted-u wordmark and a planned custom logotype.

ManyPixels
Variable and expressive type

One variable system that flexes by context, after years of neutral sans. Commissioner's flair axis does exactly this.

Onething Design · Design Shack
Warm, earthy grounds and strong contrast

Earth tones signal durability. Strong contrast matters on mobile. Hence Chalk and Ink, with AA-checked pairs.

Design & Paper · GDJ
2026 colour signals

Pantone 2026 is an airy off-white ("Cloud Dancer"), and WGSN/Coloro named "Transformative Teal". Chalk and Breath nod to both, while Pomegranate stays ownable.

Adobe · Wallpaper*
Localized systems

The core stays fixed while language and imagery adapt locally. Thumoa is bilingual by design: Greek copy is written, not translated.

Design Monks
Category whitespace

Strava owns orange, and "beast mode" brands own black and neon. Planet Fitness just refreshed its purple and yellow. A warm red on chalk is free.
