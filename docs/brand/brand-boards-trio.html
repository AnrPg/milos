One trio, five bases

The palette is now fixed: Dojo blue, Spark (Sun Lime #E4FF6E) and Heat. What changes is the base: Frost or Graphite, and Plum where it fits. Each colour comes in two tones: a deeper one for light backgrounds and a glow for dark ones, so text always passes contrast. Spark and Heat never meet in the mark.
