# Palee

Upstream identity for the member app, the gym/owner console and the marketing
site. Status: **draft v1**, proposed in the product discussion of Oct 2026 and
awaiting the founder's approval. Product context lives in
[`../product/2026-10-15-palee-product-vision-and-ia.md`](../product/2026-10-15-palee-product-vision-and-ia.md).
The visual board is [`brand-board-frost.html`](brand-board-frost.html) (locked); earlier explorations live alongside it.

## Strategy

### Overview

Palee is the social home for people who love to use their bodies, organized
around the clubs where they actually move: gyms, CrossFit boxes, dance studios,
dojos, climbing walls, run crews.

For the club (the paying customer) it is management software that turns the
place into a living community. Bookings, packages and coaching sit in the same
app the members open every day for their people and their progress. The club
also gets a door to the neighbourhood on its own terms: open events, trials and
referrals instead of discount marketplaces.

For the member it is three things in one stable app: my people (the club feed),
my club (book, train, pay, ask the coach) and me (a private journal of body and
mind).

The name is the Greek *πάλι* ("again"), spelled the way it sounds. Coming back
tomorrow is the real PR, and the app exists to bring you back. Πάλη is also
wrestling, the struggle itself. To an English ear it sounds like "pal" and
"pally", the friend you train with. Off the couch, back again, with your pals.

Palee sits in a family of plain Greek words spelled phonetically, one per app of
the studio: Mazee (μαζί) and Kefi (κέφι).

Before: a booking app you tolerate, a tracker you forget, a feed full of
strangers. After: the place where your club's people actually live.

Long-term ambition: every movement club in Europe runs on Palee, and every
member feels it is theirs.

### Audience

Primary (paying): owners of independent, community-driven movement clubs, such
as class-based gyms and boxes, dance and martial-arts schools, and studios. Then
independent coaches. They are passionate operators, not marketers, and they are
squeezed between big chains and discount marketplaces.

Primary (daily users): members aged 18–45 who train or dance several times a
week and for whom the club is part of their social life. Run-club and Hyrox
generation behaviour: small groups over follower counts.

Secondary: athletes without a club, who use the free journal and discover clubs
and events nearby.

Not for: people looking for an anonymous calorie counter, influencer-scale
fitness creators chasing global reach, or chains that want a white-label
booking widget.

### Positioning

Category: the community operating system for movement clubs.

Not a booking widget. Not a discount marketplace. Not a global follower network.
Not a calorie or macro tracker. Not a "beast mode" fitness app.

The market splits three ways: discount marketplaces (ClassPass) own the client,
global networks (Strava, Hevy) own the feed, and management software (Glofox,
PushPress, SugarWOD) owns the admin but not the life of the club. Nobody turns
the club itself into the social unit.

Structural differentials:
- The club is the social unit: verified members, channels and events, not followers
- The owner controls acquisition: open events and trial slots, not discount credits
- One stable app for people, place and self, so members open it daily
- Private by default: the journal belongs to the member, across clubs
- Works for any movement discipline, not only lifting

Territory: belonging through movement. Palee owns the feeling of walking into
your club and being known.

### Personality

Archetype: The Spirited Friend, part teammate and part coach.

Attributes: Warmth, Drive, Belonging, Playfulness, Grit, Care.

What it IS: Warm. Energetic. Inclusive. Encouraging. Human-scale. Quietly proud.

What it is NOT: Not hustle culture. Not "no pain, no gain". Not macho. Not
body-judging. Not a leaderboard of strangers. Not a cold SaaS dashboard.

### References & Anti-References

#### References

- **Duolingo:** Borrow its lovable, drawable simplicity and its sense of play in
  small moments. Do not borrow guilt-tripping streak pressure or the mascot
  shouting at you.
- **Strava clubs and kudos:** Borrow the generosity of small public
  encouragement. Do not borrow the orange, the global segment competition, or the
  endurance-only identity.
- **A Greek neighbourhood dance school or village festival (πανηγύρι):** Borrow
  people moving together in a circle, where everyone belongs regardless of level.
  Do not borrow folklore clichés: no meanders, columns or blue-and-white flags.
- **Patagonia:** Borrow values that are stated plainly and kept. Do not borrow
  the outdoor-adventure imagery.

#### Anti-References

- **Hustle and "beast mode" fitness brands:** Reject black-and-neon aggression,
  sweat-drenched grimaces, and copy about crushing or destroying.
- **Diet and body-transformation apps:** Reject before/after framing, weight as
  the hero metric, and shame-based motivation.
- **Generic SaaS:** Reject purple gradients, dashboard-as-homepage, and "all-in-one
  platform" headlines.

### Promise

Core promise:
- Your club, alive in your pocket.
- Your progress is yours, and private unless you share it.
- Owners grow on their own terms.

Base message: Palee turns movement clubs into living communities. Members
belong, owners grow, and nobody is judged for where they start.

Synthesizing phrase: Palee exists so that nobody moves alone.

### Guardrails

Tone summary: Warm, spirited, inclusive, plain-spoken.

What the brand cannot be:
- A hustle or punishment brand
- A body-shaming or diet brand
- A brand for one discipline only
- A faceless marketplace
- Anything that ranks people's bodies

Litmus test: If it would make someone feel judged about their body or their
level, it is wrong. If a dancer and a powerlifter can't both see themselves in
it, it is too narrow.

## Voice

### Identity

We are for people who move, and for the places that keep them moving. The box
at 7am, the salsa class on Thursday, the dojo, the climbing wall.

We believe the hardest part is not the workout. It is coming back tomorrow. You
come back for people, so we build around people: your club, your crew, your
coach, and a private journal that remembers how far you have come.

We are not a stopwatch and we are not a marketplace. We are the club, alive.

Essence: Nobody moves alone.

### Tagline & Slogans

Primary: **"See you palee."** Greek: **«Τα λέμε πάλι.»**

"See you palee" carries the name and the promise: the club expects you back, and
someone will be there when you come. It reads as "see you again" to Greek ears
and as "see you, pal" to English ones. The Greek line is the everyday goodbye
after class.

Tagline system (decided Oct 2026, with the rename to Palee):

| Role | English | Greek |
|---|---|---|
| Primary tagline | See you palee. | Τα λέμε πάλι. |
| In-app cheer | palee! (the kudos, "again!"): "3 palees", "Maria said palee! to your PR" | πάλι! |
| Campaign template | Raise one for… ("Raise one for the 7am crew.", "Raise one for Maria's comeback.") | stays English |
| Onboarding / name explainer | Coming back is the real PR. + *palee (πάλι): Greek for "again".* | Πάλι αύριο. |
| Owners / B2B | Members who come back, again and again. | Μέλη που έρχονται πάλι και πάλι. |
| Merch / social | Same time, palee. | Ίδια ώρα, πάλι. |

Rules:
- Put "palee!" on the kudos button and let members adopt it. Never force it into body copy.
- "Raise one" is always a toast to a person or a crew. Never show alcohol with it.
- Write the name in Latin letters, «Palee», in Greek copy too; use «πάλι» only as the everyday word.
- Retired: the previous name and its tagline (hard to say, with the wrong meaning in modern Greek), "The spirit that moves you." (not ownable), "Spirit is a team sport." (cliché).

Slogans by context:
- App store: "Your club, your people, your progress."
- Owner sales page: "Your members stay because they belong."
- Open event poster: "Open training. Everyone's welcome." (no discipline jargon on public posters)
- Journal empty state: "Day one counts. Write it down."
- Craving support screen: "Hard moment? Someone's here."

### Tonal Rules

- Talk like a friend who coaches: short, warm, direct.
- Celebrate effort and consistency, not bodies.
- Never shame, never guilt. No streak threats.
- Use "you" and "we". Never "users".
- Be bilingual by design. Greek copy is written, not translated.
- One exclamation mark per screen at most, saved for real wins.
- Plain words over jargon. Explain "WOD", "RPE" and similar the first time.
- Owners get numbers only when they are measured.

Voice by channel (same identity, different energy):

| Channel | Energy | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Push notifications | Short, warm | One idea, name a person when possible |
| Empty states | Gentle | Invite one first step |

| Owner sales | Confident, concrete | Only measured numbers |
| Investors and press | Calm, precise | Same promise, no hype |
| Sensitive moments (cravings, injury) | Quiet | No exclamation marks, no advice beyond our role |
| Errors | Plain | Own it, say what to do next |

## Visual

### Core Colors

Locked Oct 2026 on the Frost Graphite board ([`brand-board-frost.html`](brand-board-frost.html)).
Energy comes from three saturated colours; luxury comes only from the finish
(cool Frost, deep Graphite, restraint, space).

- **Frost** `#F4F5F7` (mandatory). Cool near-white page. Cards sit on it in pure
  white `#FFFFFF`; the two whites give the premium depth.
- **Graphite** `#17181C` (mandatory). Text, the mark's body, deep hero surfaces.
- **Dojo** `#2F5BFF` (mandatory). The primary: actions, navigation, the mark's
  head. Glow tone on dark: `#5B7CFF`.
- **Spark** (Sun Lime) `#E4FF6E`. What is alive right now: LIVE, new, challenges,
  palee counts. Always with Graphite text, or on Graphite. Chosen to sit clearly
  away from Nike Volt.
- **Heat** `#E0203F`. Emotion: palee! (kudos), PR, Comeback, the wordmark's dot,
  support moments. Glow tone on dark: `#FF4D6A`.
- **Plum** `#2A0F2E` (optional). Marketing, merch and the member card only;
  never in the app UI.

Usage rules:
- One deep (Graphite) hero surface per screen.
- Never Spark and Heat touching, and never both in the mark.
- Never a composition of only Spark and Graphite (reads as AEK yellow-black):
  Dojo or Heat must be in view. Spark stays a light lime, never pure yellow.
- Never a surface of only Dojo and white (reads as the national flag).
- Dark mode is "Graphite Night": Graphite page, `#22242A` cards, Frost text,
  glow tones.
- In the app the palette ships as two themes in `apps/web/src/lib/theme.ts`:
  `frost` (the default for every gym and member) and `graphite` (Graphite
  Night). Gyms and members can still pick another theme.

Retired (draft v1): Pomegranate, Chalk, Breath, Sun.

### Typefaces

- **Commissioner** (variable: weight, flair, volume), the brand voice for
  wordmark, headlines and interface. Designed by Greek type designer Kostas
  Bartsokas, with full Greek and Latin support. The flair axis lets headlines
  turn expressive while the interface stays calm.
  Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.
  Licensing: SIL Open Font License 1.1. Verified against the project repository
  (github.com/kosbarts/Commissioner, `OFL.txt`) on 2026-10-15. Free for commercial use,
  embedding and self-hosting. The font may not be sold on its own.
- **JetBrains Mono**, for numbers the system knows: scores, times, weights,
  dates. Fallback: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, monospace`.
  Licensing: SIL Open Font License 1.1, free for commercial use (per license mirrors;
  confirm in github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono before shipping). "JetBrains Mono" is a
  reserved name, so a modified copy must be renamed.

Split: Commissioner carries anything a person says. Mono carries anything that
was measured.

### Logo & Marks

- **Mark ("arms up"):** a round head between two arms raised in one thick
  curved stroke: a person cheering, the moment a PR lands and the box shouts
  «πάλι!». It also reads as an open hand and as a smile.
- **Wordmark:** lowercase "palee" followed by a round full stop in the mark's
  head colour, so the name and the mark share one shape.
- **The marker test:** the mark must be drawable by a child with one marker in
  under five seconds. No gradients, no outlines, no inner detail.
- **Locked colourway:** on Frost, arms Graphite `#17181C` and head Dojo
  `#2F5BFF`; wordmark Graphite with the full stop in Dojo. Lockup: mark left of
  the wordmark.
- Reversed (on Graphite): arms Dojo glow `#5B7CFF`, head Spark `#E4FF6E`.
  On Dojo: arms white, head Spark. This is the app icon.
- Single-colour versions (Graphite, Frost or Dojo) for stamps, embroidery and
  small sizes. Never gradient, never rotated or flipped so that the arms point
  downward, never Spark with Heat.
- Explored and parked: the earlier "dancer" mark (U3), an "eye" in the head
  and a coloured letter in the wordmark (see the Frost board labs). Source
  files: [`logo/`](logo/).

### Photography & Illustration

Mood: candid, mid-motion, sweaty-happy, together, real bodies of every shape and
age.

Subjects: groups mid-class, hands meeting after a workout, a coach laughing, a
dance circle, chalked hands, feet on a studio floor.

Avoid: lone shirtless athletes, grimacing close-ups, before/after shots, stock
models, mirror selfies as hero imagery.

Illustration: marker-style single-colour line drawings in the mark's language
(dots and thick round strokes) for empty states and onboarding.

### Art Direction

Design keywords: Warm ground. One heartbeat colour. Round thick strokes. People
in motion. Generous space. Hand-made touches.

Direction statement: Palee should feel like the moment after class when
everyone is still in the room: warm, a bit out of breath, proud, together.

Visual territory: **chalk on the studio floor.** A warm off-white ground. One
pomegranate heartbeat. Shapes simple enough to draw with a marker on a
whiteboard, the way coaches write the WOD. Numbers written in honest mono.
Nothing glossy, nothing that glows, nothing that shouts.

## Governance

### Naming

Features are named plainly in sentence case: "journal", "clubs", "check-in",
"partners". No coined suffixes (Pro, Plus, AI, Max).

### Trademark status

Preliminary screen (2026-10-21), **not a legal clearance**: no registered mark,
app or company named "Palee" was found. Nearby: US mark PAL (PAL App Limited,
class 9, software for meeting people for activities) and PALLY (India, class 9).
palee.app, palee.club and palee.fit were unregistered on 2026-10-21; palee.com
is taken. Before launch, run a professional clearance in EUIPO/TMview, WIPO
Global Brand Database, USPTO and the Greek registry for Classes 9, 41, 42 and
44, have a lawyer assess PAL before any US launch, and file in the EU and Greece
first.

### Claims

Approved: none yet.

Draft candidates (need evidence before use):
- "Members of clubs with active channels stay longer." Needs a retention
  measurement.
- "Check in with one scan." Needs the shipped QR flow.

Prohibited: any weight-loss or body-change claim. Any health or medical claim.
Any retention percentage not measured on our own data.

### Accessibility Commitments

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA on every surface. Brand text pairs: Graphite on Frost
16.3:1. Dojo on white 5.2:1 and on Frost 4.7:1 (AA). Heat on white 4.7:1 (AA);
on Frost 4.3:1, so Heat text sits on white cards or is large only. Graphite on
Spark 15.9:1. White on Dojo 5.2:1, white on Heat 4.7:1.

Beyond the target: every core flow is usable one-handed, and celebration motion
respects reduced-motion settings.
