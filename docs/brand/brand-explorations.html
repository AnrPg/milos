Tagline lab, five boards, eight bold moves

The same mark, «ο χορευτής», carried into five very different worlds, so you can choose by feel. Each tagline is scored for ownability (would it still work for Strava, ClassPass or Nike?), Greek fit and range across disciplines.

01 · Tagline lab

Five territories, eighteen lines, and how ownable each one is

The score combines three tests. Ownability: does it break if you put a competitor's name in front of it? Greek fit: does it sound natural in Greek, not translated? Range: does it work for a dancer and a powerlifter alike?

RecommendationPrimary: «Με ψυχή. Μαζί.» / "With heart. Together."It names thumos in plain words ("ψυχή" is the everyday Greek for spirit and heart), adds the club, and is short enough for a stamp or sticker. Keep "Nobody moves alone." as the movement line for campaigns, and "Raise one." as the interaction (the kudos gesture).
02 · Brand boards

Five worlds for the same mark

Pick one, or mix: for example the palette of one with the type of another. Each board has its own palette, type pairing (all with Greek), mood, poster, app icons and a straight pros and cons.

Eight ideas to stretch the brand
