Making the green ours

Our current Spark (#C8FF1E) is almost identical to Nike Volt: a colour distance of 4, where under 10 the eye barely sees a difference. Below are seven variants, each placed in the same mockups next to Dojo, Heat, Graphite, Frost and Plum. The bar shows how far each one moves from Volt; above 25, it reads as a different colour.

DecisionSun Lime #E4FF6EChosen: sunny, Mediterranean and clearly away from Nike Volt (29). Guardrail against the AEK yellow-black reading: never a composition of only Sun Lime and graphite. Dojo or Heat is always in view, and Sun Lime stays a light lime, never pushed to pure yellow.
